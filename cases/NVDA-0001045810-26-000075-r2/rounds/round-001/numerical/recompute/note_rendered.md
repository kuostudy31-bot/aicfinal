## Results

**Reported.** Revenue for FY2027 Q2 was $96.2bn (17.9% q/q, 105.9% y/y), bringing trailing-twelve-month revenue to $303.0bn. Gross margin was 75.0%. Management described margins this way: “GAAP and non-GAAP gross margins were approximately flat sequentially as our Blackwell architecture remains the vast majority of our revenue.” Operating margin was 66.2%, net income $59.7bn and diluted EPS $2.46. Free cash flow (operating cash flow less capital expenditure) was $21.4bn for the quarter.

**Balance sheet and working capital (reported, from the CFO commentary).** “Inventory was $31.6 billion, up from $25.8 billion sequentially, as we prepare for the introduction of Vera Rubin in the third quarter.” “Accounts receivable was $63.1 billion with 60 days sales outstanding (DSO), up from 45 days sequentially, due to extended payment terms on large, multi-quarter agreements with certain investment-grade customers.” Capital return continued: “During the second quarter of fiscal 2027, NVIDIA returned approximately $26.0 billion to shareholders in the form of shares repurchased and cash dividends.”

**Net cash (code output).** Net cash is $66.0bn. It is cash and cash equivalents plus current marketable securities, less total debt (the debt figure includes both the short-term and long-term portions). Current marketable securities now include both marketable debt securities and marketable equity securities. **Judgment:** the marketable equity securities are carried at fair value, so they move with market prices, and their realisable value at any later date may differ materially from the balance-sheet figure. Non-current non-marketable investments and operating lease liabilities are left out of net cash. On the asset side that is conservative.

## Guidance and outlook

**Company guidance for FY2027Q3 (quoted):**

- Revenue: “Revenue is expected to be $108.0 billion, plus or minus 2%.”
- Gross margin: “GAAP and non-GAAP gross margins are expected to be 74.0%, plus or minus 50 basis points.”
- Operating expenses: “GAAP and non-GAAP operating expenses are expected to be approximately $9.2 billion and $9.0 billion, respectively.”
- Tax rate: “For the full year fiscal 2027, we expect GAAP and non-GAAP tax rates to be between 16.0% and 18.0%, excluding any discrete items and material changes to our tax environment.”
- China: “We are not assuming any Data Center compute revenue from China in our outlook.”

**Our forecast.** FY2027Q3 revenue is $108.0bn, which is the guidance midpoint ($108.0bn). Gross margin is the 74.0% midpoint, and the full-year tax rate is the 17.0% midpoint of the guided range. Operating expenses follow the GAAP guide of $9.2bn (non-GAAP $9.0bn). That gives forecast FY2027Q3 EPS of $2.42.

**Judgment.** We have no company guidance for the quarters after FY2027Q3. For those quarters we extend the recent deceleration in sequential revenue growth, hold gross margin at the guided level and let operating expenses grow more slowly than revenue. These are analyst judgments, not guidance. Like the company's outlook, our forecast assumes no China Data Center compute revenue in any quarter. Any resumption of those sales would be upside to the model; none is assumed. The guided step-down in gross margin from the 75.0% reported coincides with the Vera Rubin introduction. We assume neither further dilution nor a recovery.

## Model and valuation

**Forecast.** NTM revenue is $492.4bn and NTM EPS is $11.10. The model applies the guided tax rate to operating income and assumes no other income or expense. It ignores interest income and gains or losses on investments, including the fair-value changes on marketable equity securities.

**Valuation (code output).** The target price of $186 blends two methods, with the larger weight on the DCF:

- A DCF value of $141.68 per share. The terminal value makes up 71% of DCF enterprise value.
- A peer cross-check that applies the peer median TTM EV/Sales of 23.0x (AMD, Broadcom, Marvell) to NVIDIA's TTM revenue of $303.0bn.

Net cash of $66.0bn is added to enterprise value. Against the last close of $209.66, the implied upside is -11.3%, which gives a rating of Underperform.

**Judgment.** The discount rate, the long-run FCF margins and the terminal growth rate are analyst assumptions. Market-rate inputs are not in the case snapshot, so the discount rate is built from assumed risk-free, beta and equity-risk-premium inputs. Terminal value carries most of the DCF, and the upside is close to the band boundary. A modestly lower discount rate would therefore move the result from Underperform into Neutral. We do not regard the rating as a high-conviction call. The peer multiple sets NVIDIA's revenue against companies with lower margins. The peers' latest reported trailing-twelve-month periods also end one to three months before NVIDIA's quarter end. For both reasons the peer cross-check gets the smaller weight.

## Risks

- **Commitments and guarantees (reported):** “Our commitments increased from $119 billion last quarter to $279 billion, primarily related to the procurement of memory.” “Our guarantee obligations are capped at a total of $105 billion and become effective in phases as certain conditions are met, including data centers becoming ready for service, with the first expected in fiscal 2029.” **Judgment:** these magnify the effect of any slowdown in AI infrastructure spending on cash flow and margins.
- **Customer concentration and credit terms:** “Accounts receivable was $63.1 billion with 60 days sales outstanding (DSO), up from 45 days sequentially, due to extended payment terms on large, multi-quarter agreements with certain investment-grade customers.” **Judgment:** the longer payment terms on large multi-quarter agreements tie up working capital and point to dependence on a small set of very large customers.
- **China and export controls:** the outlook assumes no China Data Center compute revenue (“We are not assuming any Data Center compute revenue from China in our outlook.”). Further restrictions or changes to them remain a two-way risk.
- **Product transition:** the Vera Rubin ramp (“Inventory was $31.6 billion, up from $25.8 billion sequentially, as we prepare for the introduction of Vera Rubin in the third quarter.”) brings execution and margin risk. This is already visible in the guided gross-margin step-down to 74.0%.
- **Valuation and model risk (judgment):** terminal value is 71% of DCF enterprise value, and the rating sits near a band boundary. Net cash includes marketable equity securities at fair value, and their value moves with market prices.
- **Upside risks (judgment):** our growth after FY2027Q3 could prove too conservative. China data-center sales could resume. Gross margin could recover after the transition.
