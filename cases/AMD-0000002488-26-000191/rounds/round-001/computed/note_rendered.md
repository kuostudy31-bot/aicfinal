# AMD FY2026 Q3 earnings update: Underperform, target price $265

## Key points

- Reported: revenue for FY2026 Q3 (CY2026Q3, quarter ended 2026-09-26) was $13.4bn, up 16.0% quarter on quarter and 44.7% year on year. Diluted EPS was $1.46.
- Company guidance: management guides the next quarter to revenue of about $17.0bn: “For the fourth quarter of 2026, AMD expects revenue to be approximately $17 billion, plus or minus 3%.” It cites Data Center strength: “Demand for our Instinct and EPYC products continued to strengthen, and we expect growth to accelerate further in the fourth quarter as we scale Helios and ‘Venice’ production.”
- Our forecast: FY2026Q4 revenue of $17.0bn and EPS of $2.34; next-twelve-month (NTM) revenue of $73.1bn and EPS of $10.11.
- Our valuation: target price $265 against a last close of $483.90, an implied return of -45.2%, so we rate the shares Underperform. Our judgment is that the share price already prices in more sustained growth and margin expansion than our forecast assumes.

## Results

Reported figures below are from the company's 10-Q XBRL data for FY2026 Q3 / CY2026Q3.

- Revenue: $13.4bn (16.0% quarter on quarter, 44.7% year on year); trailing-twelve-month revenue $45.4bn.
- Margins (GAAP): gross margin 53.9% and operating margin 21.2%. The 10-Q attributes the year-on-year gross margin improvement to mix: “Gross margin was 54% and 52% for the three months ended September 26, 2026 and September 27, 2025, respectively, where the increase was driven by a favorable product mix, including higher Data Center segment revenue.”
- Net income $2.4bn; diluted EPS $1.46.
- Free cash flow (operating cash flow less capital expenditure) was $0.6bn in the quarter. Our reading of the filing is that working capital built for the Data Center ramp, mainly inventory and receivables, absorbed much of the operating cash flow.

## Guidance and outlook

Company guidance for the next quarter, quoted from the earnings release exhibits:

- Revenue: “For the fourth quarter of 2026, AMD expects revenue to be approximately $17 billion, plus or minus 3%.” Midpoint $17.0bn.
- Gross margin (non-GAAP): “Non-GAAP gross margin is expected to be approximately 55%.” Midpoint 55.0%. This is a non-GAAP figure; the reported margin above is GAAP.
- Operating expenses (non-GAAP): “Operating Expenses ~$3.75 Billion”
- Tax rate: “Effective Tax Rate 13% of pre-tax income” (13.0%).
- Share count: “Diluted Share Count(2) ~1.67 Billion shares”

Our forecast (analyst assumptions, GAAP basis): we set FY2026Q4 revenue at the guidance midpoint, giving $17.0bn. We converted the non-GAAP margin and opex guidance to GAAP by adding back the reconciling items in the latest quarter's reconciliation. The result is EPS of $2.34. For the three quarters after that, our judgment is that the growth rate will not keep accelerating. We assume a seasonally soft first quarter followed by a slower Data Center ramp. This gives NTM revenue of $73.1bn and NTM EPS of $10.11. We apply the guided tax rate and assume no repeat of the discrete stock-compensation tax benefits that lowered recent GAAP tax rates.

## Model and valuation

The target price of $265 blends two methods, weighted mainly to the DCF:

- Discounted cash flow (DCF): $185.79 per share. Terminal value accounts for 73% of enterprise value, so the result depends heavily on the long-run growth and discount-rate assumptions. The discount rate, terminal growth and free-cash-flow margin path are analyst judgments; the inputs file gives the reasons for each.
- Peer EV/Sales: the median trailing EV/Sales of the selected compute and data-center silicon peers is 16.3x. We use it as a cross-check from market prices.
- Net cash of $8.7bn (cash and short-term investments less debt) is added to enterprise value.

The blended value gives a target price of $265 against the last close of $483.90 (-45.2%). Under the policy bands that is Underperform. In our judgment the gap mainly reflects the DCF: on our free-cash-flow assumptions, the current price needs either faster sustained growth or higher long-run margins than we model. The rating is therefore sensitive to those assumptions, and a higher growth path or lower discount rate would narrow the gap.

## Risks

Our judgments on the main risks to the forecast and target price are below.

- Upside risk to our target: Data Center demand and the Helios rack-scale ramp could run ahead of our forecast, which assumes slower growth after the guided quarter (“Demand for our Instinct and EPYC products continued to strengthen, and we expect growth to accelerate further in the fourth quarter as we scale Helios and ‘Venice’ production.”).
- Export controls: “This restriction impacts our AMD Instinct™ MI308 products.” Further restrictions or license changes could cut Data Center revenue.
- Dilution: warrants issued to customers are not included in our diluted share base. If they vest, value per share would fall. Pending acquisitions are also not in our share count or net cash.
- Customer concentration and the cyclical nature of AI capital spending, together with a year-on-year decline in Client and Gaming revenue reported in the release.
- Valuation method: with terminal value at 73% of DCF enterprise value, small changes in the discount rate or terminal growth move the target materially. The peer median also depends on which companies are included.
