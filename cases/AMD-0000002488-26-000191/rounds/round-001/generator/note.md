## Model and valuation

The target price of {{target_price}} blends two methods, weighted mainly to the DCF:

- Discounted cash flow (DCF): {{dcf_per_share}} per share. Terminal value accounts for {{terminal_share}} of enterprise value, so the result depends heavily on the long-run growth and discount-rate assumptions. The discount rate, terminal growth and free-cash-flow margin path are analyst judgments; the inputs file gives the reasons for each.
- Peer EV/Sales: the median trailing EV/Sales of the selected compute and data-center silicon peers is {{peer_ev_sales}}. We use it as a cross-check from market prices.
- Net cash of {{net_cash}} (cash and short-term investments less debt) is added to enterprise value.

The blended value gives a target price of {{target_price}} against the last close of {{price}} ({{upside}}). Under the policy bands that is {{rating}}. In our judgment the gap mainly reflects the DCF: on our free-cash-flow assumptions, the current price needs either faster sustained growth or higher long-run margins than we model. The rating is therefore sensitive to those assumptions, and a higher growth path or lower discount rate would narrow the gap.

## Risks

Our judgments on the main risks to the forecast and target price are below.

- Upside risk to our target: Data Center demand and the Helios rack-scale ramp could run ahead of our forecast, which assumes slower growth after the guided quarter ([[Q-6]]).
- Export controls: [[Q-8]] Further restrictions or license changes could cut Data Center revenue.
- Dilution: warrants issued to customers are not included in our diluted share base. If they vest, value per share would fall. Pending acquisitions are also not in our share count or net cash.
- Customer concentration and the cyclical nature of AI capital spending, together with a year-on-year decline in Client and Gaming revenue reported in the release.
- Valuation method: with terminal value at {{terminal_share}} of DCF enterprise value, small changes in the discount rate or terminal growth move the target materially. The peer median also depends on which companies are included.
