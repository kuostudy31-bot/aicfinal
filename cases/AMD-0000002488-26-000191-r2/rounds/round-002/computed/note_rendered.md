# AMD FY2026 Q3 earnings update: Underperform, target price $297

## Key points

- **Reported:** FY2026 Q3 (calendar CY2026Q3, quarter ended 2026-09-26) revenue was $13.4bn, up 44.7% year on year and 16.0% quarter on quarter; diluted EPS was $1.46.
- **Company guidance:** management guides next-quarter revenue to about $17.0bn and non-GAAP gross margin to about 55.0%: “For the fourth quarter of 2026, AMD expects revenue to be approximately $17 billion, plus or minus 3%.” “Non-GAAP gross margin is expected to be approximately 55%.”
- **Our forecast:** we model FY2026Q4 revenue of $17.0bn (anchored on the guidance midpoint) and EPS of $2.26; next-twelve-month revenue is $72.6bn and EPS $9.72.
- **Our judgment:** the growth story is strong, but at $483.90 the shares already discount far more than our cash-flow model supports. Our blended target of $297 implies -38.6% versus the last close, which maps to Underperform under the policy bands.

## Results

All figures in this section are reported results from the FY2026 Q3 10-Q; the financial figures are derived by code from SEC XBRL data, and quoted text is taken from the 10-Q.

- Revenue: $13.4bn (44.7% year on year, 16.0% quarter on quarter); trailing-twelve-month revenue $45.4bn.
- GAAP gross margin: 53.9%. The company attributes the year-on-year improvement to mix: “Gross margin was 54% and 52% for the three months ended September 26, 2026 and September 27, 2025, respectively, where the increase was driven by a favorable product mix, including higher Data Center segment revenue.”
- GAAP operating margin: 21.2%.
- Net income: $2.4bn; diluted EPS $1.46. Reported earnings include two large one-off items that act in opposite directions. On the tax line, the 10-Q states: “In addition, the tax benefit for the three and nine months ended September 26, 2026 also included a discrete tax benefit related to stock-based compensation of $772 million and $918 million, respectively.” In other income (expense), it states: “During the three and nine months ended September 26, 2026, the Company recorded a noncash charge of $1.2 billion, primarily related to a loss from remeasurement of the warrant liability based on the change in fair value of the warrants within Other income (expense), net.” Our judgment: the discrete tax benefit raises reported EPS and the noncash warrant charge lowers it. The 10-Q does not give the after-tax effect of the warrant charge, so the net effect of the two items on the recurring run rate cannot be determined from the filing; we assess recurring earnings excluding both items rather than from reported EPS.
- Free cash flow (operating cash flow less capital expenditure): $0.6bn, held back by a build in inventories and receivables.
- Net cash at quarter end: $8.7bn.

## Guidance and outlook

Company guidance (quoted from the earnings release):

- Revenue: “For the fourth quarter of 2026, AMD expects revenue to be approximately $17 billion, plus or minus 3%.”
- Margin: “Non-GAAP gross margin is expected to be approximately 55%.” Note that this is a non-GAAP measure; our model forecasts GAAP gross margin, which runs below the non-GAAP figure because of acquisition-related amortization and stock-based compensation in cost of sales.
- Demand commentary: “we expect growth to accelerate further in the fourth quarter as we scale Helios and ‘Venice’ production” and “Our fourth-quarter outlook reflects accelerating revenue and earnings growth, driven by strong demand across our Data Center portfolio.”

Our forecasts (from code, using our assumptions): FY2026Q4 revenue of $17.0bn, essentially at the guided $17.0bn midpoint, and EPS of $2.26. For the following quarters we assume a seasonally soft first quarter of FY2027 and a resumption of growth thereafter, giving next-twelve-month revenue of $72.6bn and EPS of $9.72.

Our judgment: the guided step-up rests on a rack-scale (Helios) and server CPU ramp that is lumpy by nature; we deliberately grow more slowly than the recent sequential trend after the guided quarter.

## Model and valuation

- **Method (our assumptions):** the target blends a discounted cash flow (majority weight) with a peer EV/Sales cross-check (NVDA, AVGO, MRVL, INTC) applied to trailing revenue.
- **DCF:** the discounted cash flow gives $195.37 per share; the terminal value is 75% of enterprise value, so the result is sensitive to the discount rate and terminal growth assumptions. Free-cash-flow margins are set between the trailing level and the FY2025 peak and rise modestly as growth slows (analyst judgment).
- **Peers:** the peer median EV/Sales is 16.3x, a sector multiple that embeds AI-compute growth expectations beyond our five-year horizon.
- **Net cash** of $8.7bn is added to enterprise value.
- **Result:** blended target price $297 versus the $483.90 close on 2026-11-03, i.e. -38.6% implied return, hence **Underperform**.

Our judgment: the gap between the share price and our DCF value means the market is pricing either materially higher long-run margins or a longer high-growth period than we assume. We would revisit the rating if free-cash-flow conversion improves materially as the working-capital build reverses.

## Risks

- **Upside risks to our view (our judgment):** faster-than-modelled data-center ramp (“Our fourth-quarter outlook reflects accelerating revenue and earnings growth, driven by strong demand across our Data Center portfolio.”), sustained gross-margin expansion from mix, and sector multiples staying elevated, which would lift the peer-based leg of the valuation.
- **Downside risks:** export controls and trade measures, as the company itself notes: “Government actions and regulations, including export controls, national-security-based regulations, import tariffs and trade protection measures, may limit our ability to export our products to certain customers, increase costs, harm our competitive position and materially affect our business.”
- Customer concentration and lumpy system shipments can make quarterly revenue volatile.
- Continued working-capital build could keep free-cash-flow conversion below our forecast.
- Valuation risk: a large share of DCF value (75%) sits in the terminal value.
